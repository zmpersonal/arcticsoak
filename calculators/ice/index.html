How much ice do you need for a cold plunge?

For 100 gallons starting at 70°F, the theoretical requirement is about 103 lb of ice to reach 50°F, 133 lb to reach 45°F, or 171 lb to reach 39°F.

Theoretical ice requirement
103 lb

About 11 × 10-lb bags.

Approximate bag cost: $38.50.

Assumes ice begins near 32°F and remains in the tub. Sun, air, plumbing, warm walls, drainage and imperfect mixing increase real-world requirements.

The heat-balance formula

Water weighs about 8.34 lb per gallon and requires roughly 1 BTU to cool 1 lb by 1°F. Ice near 32°F absorbs about 144 BTU/lb while melting, then the meltwater warms to the target temperature.

ice lb = [gallons × 8.34 × (start°F − target°F)] ÷ [144 + (target°F − 32)]
100-gallon example: 100 × 8.34 × (70 − 50) ÷ (144 + 18) = approximately 103 lb of ice. With 10-lb bags, round up to 11 bags.
