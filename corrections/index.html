Corrections policy

Show us where the record breaks.

Useful correction reports identify the exact page or field, expected value, supporting source and why the change matters.

What to include

  • URL, city slug or dataset row.
  • Field name and current value.
  • Proposed value and a primary source where possible.
  • Retrieval date, station ID, EIA period or equipment test condition.
  • Whether the issue changes a headline conclusion or only metadata.

How corrections are handled

Source errors are fixed in the current dataset. Material formula or constant changes require a model-version increment and changelog entry. Historical release snapshots remain available so older citations can be interpreted.

Contact route

Until a public submission form is added, correction reports can be sent through the public repository’s issue tracker associated with this site. Do not include private account, medical or payment information.
