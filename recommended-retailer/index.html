Why equipment details matter

Two 100-gallon plunges in the same city can use very different amounts of electricity. Insulation, cover fit, pump schedule, chiller capacity, plumbing and direct-sun exposure often matter more than a small difference in local weather.

Before buying, ask for delivered cooling capacity at your summer ambient temperature—not only nominal horsepower. Also confirm the lowest supported set point, recommended water volume, flow requirements, operating range, sanitation setup and warranty coverage.

Buyer checklist

Questions to answer before ordering

01 · CLIMATE

Where will it run?

Indoor, garage, outdoor shade and direct sun create materially different heat loads.

02 · CAPACITY

What must the chiller deliver?

Use BTU/hr at expected ambient and water temperatures, not horsepower alone.

03 · BASE LOAD

What runs continuously?

Pumps, filtration, ozone or UV can dominate annual energy in cooler climates.

04 · SUPPORT

Who handles problems?

Confirm warranty terms, replacement parts, freight procedure and installation requirements.
